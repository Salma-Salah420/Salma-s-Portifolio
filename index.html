AI Job Scheduling System

Job scheduling is a critical problem in computer science and operations research, where the goal is to allocate tasks (jobs) to resources (machines or processors) efficiently while respecting constraints such as deadlines, dependencies, and resource capacities. Efficient scheduling reduces overall execution time, maximizes resource utilization, and ensures timely completion of tasks. This project focuses on solving the job scheduling problem using two approaches: Genetic Algorithm (GA) – a heuristic, evolutionary technique inspired by natural selection. Backtracking Algorithm – a systematic, exhaustive search algorithm used for finding all feasible solutions. The combination allows us to compare an approximate solution (GA) with an exact solution (Backtracking) and understand the trade-offs between speed and optimality.
